Privacy Policy - Tic-Tac-Toe

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tic-Tac-Toe, developed by Patrick O'Connor, handles information when you use the application. The application provides single-player and two-player Tic-Tac-Toe gameplay.

2. Information Collection and Use

The application does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or to developer-operated servers.

Gameplay is processed locally on your device. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to play.

3. Device Information

The application does not intentionally collect or transmit personal device information, such as precise location, contacts, or device identifiers, for tracking or identification purposes.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or share personal information collected through the application. The application does not send gameplay information to developer-operated servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The application does not intentionally use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The application is distributed through third-party platforms, such as Google Play, which may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Storage and Security

The application does not operate a developer-managed server or database for user information. Gameplay is processed locally on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to provide personal information to play.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, together with its revision date.
